# RoosterViewCall - Project Context

## Product Overview

RoosterViewCall is a modern Queue & Kitchen Management System designed for restaurants, cafés, food trucks, bakeries, and fast-food businesses.

The system manages the complete customer service flow, from ticket generation to kitchen preparation and public order display.

This project is the flagship software product of RoosterCode and is intended to demonstrate enterprise-level software engineering practices.

---

# Vision

Create an intuitive, modern, scalable, and AI-assisted queue management platform that can be deployed by small and medium-sized food businesses.

The product should be visually attractive, easy to use, and designed with an exceptional user experience.

RoosterViewCall is not only a software application.

It is also the reference architecture and development methodology for future RoosterCode products.

---

# Mission

Provide food businesses with a modern solution that improves customer experience, reduces waiting confusion, increases operational efficiency, and gives managers complete visibility over their operation.

# Target Users

The application has six user roles.

## Administrator

Responsible for system configuration.

Responsibilities:

- Manage users
- Configure business
- Configure printers
- Configure displays
- Configure ticket numbering
- View reports

---

## Cashier

Responsible for customer service.

Responsibilities:

- Create orders
- Print tickets
- Receive payments
- Update order status

---

## Kitchen

Responsible for food preparation.

Responsibilities:

- View pending orders
- Start preparation
- Complete preparation
- Notify finished orders

---

## Public Display

Displays information to customers.

Responsibilities:

- Show completed orders
- Show orders in preparation
- Play promotional content
- Play notification sounds

---

## Supervisor

Responsible for monitoring the operation.

Responsibilities:

- View dashboards
- Analyze metrics
- Monitor kitchen performance
- Monitor waiting times

---

## Customer

Interacts only through the public interface.

Responsibilities:

- Receive ticket
- Wait for order
- Pick up food

# Public Display

The public display is one of the core features.

The screen is divided into two sections.

## Left Side

Promotional content.

Examples:

- Food promotions
- Videos
- Images
- Seasonal offers
- Brand identity

## Right Side

Order status.

Display:

- Orders in preparation
- Orders ready for pickup
- Highlight newly completed orders
- Play notification sounds
- Smooth animations
