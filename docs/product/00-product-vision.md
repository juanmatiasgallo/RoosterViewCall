# Product Vision

## Objective

RoosterViewCall is a modern Queue & Kitchen Management System for restaurants, cafés, food trucks, bakeries, and fast-food businesses.

It manages the complete customer service flow, from ticket generation to kitchen preparation and public order display.

---

## Problem

Small and medium food businesses typically rely on:

- Paper tickets or shouted order numbers
- Improvised whiteboards in the kitchen
- No visibility into preparation or waiting times
- No data on operational performance

This creates confusion for customers, inefficiency in the kitchen, and no actionable data for managers.

---

## Solution

Centralize the entire order lifecycle in a single platform:

- Ticket generation at the point of sale
- Order tracking from creation to pickup
- A real-time kitchen board for preparation
- A public display showing order status and promotional content
- A dashboard with operational metrics and statistics
- Docker-based deployment for easy installation on-site

---

## Target Audience

First release:

- Cafés
- Coffee Shops
- Food Trucks
- Restaurants
- Burger Shops
- Pizza Shops
- Bakeries
- Small Fast Food Businesses

Future versions may extend to hospitals, pharmacies, government offices, banks, and other queue-driven public or private institutions.

---

## Goals

- Easy to use, with minimal training required for staff
- Visually attractive, modern, and responsive on all devices
- Reliable enough for continuous daily operation
- Deployable via Docker with minimal infrastructure requirements
- Built as a scalable foundation, not a single-purpose tool
- Designed to reduce customer waiting confusion and improve perceived service quality
- Designed to give supervisors real visibility into kitchen and service performance
